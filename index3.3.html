1. College & Course Introduction

Welcome to the Department of Computer Science & Engineering. Our program is designed to provide students with a strong theoretical foundation and practical skills in software development, data structures, algorithm design, and modern web technologies. We aim to foster innovation, critical thinking, and technical mastery to prepare students for top industry roles and graduate studies.

2. Departments & Core Subjects

Our department offers comprehensive training across various core domains:

  • Computer Science & Engineering
  • Data Science & Artificial Intelligence
  • Information Technology
  • Cyber Security
  • Software Engineering

3. Admission Process

Follow these step-by-step guidelines to secure your admission:

  1. Submit the online application form before the deadline.
  2. Upload required qualification documents and academic transcripts.
  3. Appear for the entrance examination / personal interview.
  4. Check the merit list published on the official college website.
  5. Complete fee payment and document verification to confirm seat allocation.
